# APEX v0.7.0.1 — Fuel Consumption & Mass Dynamics (experimental 043)

Base v0.7.0 PR #174, merge 381b2a7ed3e082f05367ff56fd5c629206f51074, FAST 45913526730 PASS (57 scripts, 83.441 powertrain checks, 161/117 shifts Monza/Silverstone). 042 is opt-in; 041 remains default until dedicated activation. This increment adds 043 opt-in, never changes legacy 040/041/042. Candidate, not release.

- Each car has an independent fuel ledger in kg, initial load from nominal race duration (0.028 kg/s + 8 kg reserve), dry mass 642 kg, remaining fuel, consumed fuel, current mass and last estimated V8 work. Reference parameters are experimental, not calibrated 2012 fuel loads.
- Estimated shaft work from actual solved distance, road resistance and change in kinetic energy, divided by transmission efficiency. Existing KERS offer offsets shaft demand at most; no duplicate debit. kW*s=kJ, J/1000=kJ, LHV 43,000 kJ/kg, burn=shaft energy/(efficiency*LHV), idle minimum 0.001 kg/s. 0.28 attack, 0.30 normal, 0.32 conserve. No energy creation or negative mass; fuel economy uses existing pilot pace, no new command or random event.
- Mass changes the force-limited positive drive, reference acceleration/brake rates and apex preview. 043 substitutes physical mass for legacy synthetic fuel lap-index pace bonus; older paths retain it. Detailed tyre load transfer stays in 0.7.1.
- Launch uses work proxy from displacement; pit uses idle estimate, no refuel. Retirement occurs on exhaustion at end of tick, or on safe pit exit to preserve garage queue. Real finish crossing wins at tick boundary. DNF stops progress, no fake finish timestamp, text label and no championship points (initial gameplay rule, not full FIA 90% classification implementation). All-retired race closes without fictitious winner; GUI/3D DNF handling and points edge cases require further review.
- Tests: dimensional 1000x regression, mass balance, energy, KERS, conservation modes, acceleration/brake oracles, 24 cars dry/wet, replay seek, RNG and recording parity, old 042 without fuel, forced-exhaustion fixture. Separate 042 V21 continuous test to support later activation; no fake progress. Long 043 race and full calibration, pit retirement edges, native benchmark, HUD integration and FULL remain pending. Preserve all subsequent roadmap, including Godot+C++ after 0.7.2. No C++ migration yet.
