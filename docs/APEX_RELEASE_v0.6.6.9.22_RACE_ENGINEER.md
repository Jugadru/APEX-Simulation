# APEX v0.6.6.9.22 — Race Engineer 4.0 (integrated into main)

Base: 9.21, sporting revision 040. All new communication is observational/presentation-only. Existing manual, assisted and automatic authority stays unchanged. No new pit, pace or KERS commands are generated by the radio.

## Implemented
- Contextual diagnosis of six driver errors, tyres, temperature, weather, dirty air and traffic. Qualitative confidence, sustained observations and follow-ups; interrupted recovery does not count as completed.
- Bounded per-driver memory and common typed message format; automatic legacy engine radio joins the priority/expiry queue, explicit queries are delivered synchronously through the same history and audio route. Suppression of configuration/mode messages; engine records retained.
- Fictional CALM/TECHNICAL/DEADPAN engineer styles, original English banter and nearby confirmed-pass information. Banter is gated by driver pressure, confidence and familiarity; no unsupported rival identity or invented damage.
- Existing fictional STAFF contracts provide identity, skill level and voice profile. Familiarity stored in staff_soul per engineer/driver pair, bounded and settled once per completed championship GP. Single races and replay do not settle career data. No sporting bonus.
- Radio HUD: contextual questions, engineer identity, diagnostic confidence, relationship display, optional local voice, independent volume, PT subtitles. No new command authority.
- 179 Radio 3.0 original phrase translations plus new contextual catalogue and dynamic lap/strategy translations. Unknown phrases are not guessed.
- Optional OS TTS via Godot DisplayServer; bounded audio queue, priority interruption, high-speed low-priority suppression and text fallback. No cloned recordings, guaranteed accent or voice availability. OS speech is not routed through a Godot radio filter; dedicated recorded-voice/effects production remains outside verified scope.
